Each person who asks sends 1.25 questions. Merging similar questions leaves ~0.64 drafts per question, and 10% of drafts are regenerated. Every draft is priced at $0.049. The budget line = expected + ~$1–2 for question triage (~$0.0005 per question) + ~$20 November preparation, rounded. The high scenario is covered by the contingency together with the daily cap. Mentor-assist drafts (~$0.025) and refused or medical questions (~$0.008, or $0 when stopped before any AI call) cost less, so the real bill should be lower.

Assumptions
  • 3,000 delegates (expected) and 5,000 (headroom); most questions are asked on the 4 event days.
  • AI: Gemini Pro-class draft + Flash-class check, December price ($4 input / $20 output per 1M tokens for the draft model, double the November price). Measured prompt sizes; output sizes from live pilot drafts.
  • Firestore: ~10 writes per registration and per question. Reads are mostly from the staff desk, which reads every record on each refresh (~1,200 refreshes in the event period). Roughly 0.1–0.2 M writes and 8–13 M reads in total.
  • Email: ~3 emails per delegate (sign-up code + resend, a later sign-in code, answer-ready notice).
  • After the event: ~10% of event-time question volume per month; Vercel back on Hobby; email on the free tier.
  • ₹88 = $1 (assumption). Prices excluding GST (18% may apply).
How to keep costs low
  • Merging: similar questions are grouped, so one AI draft answers many students (~36% fewer drafts).
  • Daily cap: AI drafting stops automatically at the cap ($30/day in the event, $5–10/day otherwise); mentors still answer by hand.
  • Flash fallback: switching drafts to the Flash model cuts AI cost by ~75% (≈ $0.012 per draft) if the cap is near.
  • Vertex AI credits: bill AI to WAC's Google Cloud account so existing credits pay for it.
  • Downgrade after the event: Vercel Pro → Hobby and Resend Pro → free in January 2027.
  • Pre-draft the 368 model answers before 1 December (~$9 instead of ~$18).
  • Budget alerts: Google Cloud / Firebase (e.g. ₹2,000/month) and Vercel spend management.

3. Notes

  • Cheaper email alternative: Amazon SES costs about $0.10 per 1,000 emails, so ~$1–2 for the whole event instead of $40. However, it needs an AWS account, approval to leave the SES sandbox (often 1–2 days), and the domain's DNS records. It can plug in through the app's existing SMTP option. Choose SES if WAC already has AWS; otherwise Resend Pro is the faster, safer path.
  • Spending controls are part of the plan: DISHA AI has a hard daily cap in the staff desk. Vercel Pro and Google Cloud both have spend alerts. No service is open-ended.
  • Separate from this budget — maintenance and support effort (not infrastructure): plan for one developer on call during 10–13 December, about 4–8 hours a week in November for final testing and fixes, and about 2–4 hours a month afterwards. WAC can budget this separately.
