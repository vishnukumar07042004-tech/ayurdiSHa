AYURDISHA — Features & Specifications

The digital Meet the Mentors hall, built for the Meet the Mentors programme (Experience Zone 7) of the 11th World Ayurveda Congress, Bhubaneswar. A short guide to what the app does, so the WAC team can test it.

Live app: https://ayushmarg.vercel.app
Ask Desk (QR link): ayushmarg.vercel.app/ask?src=qr
Staff area: staff access code shared separately

At a glance

What it is
A website where career seekers ask one career question and receive a written answer that a mentor or WAC official has checked and approved.
Who uses it
Seekers (Ayurveda students, interns, PG and PhD scholars, researchers, practitioners; in India or abroad), staff and officials (curation and review) and mentors (answer through staff; no login).
Meet DISHA
DISHA — the AYURDISHA AI mentor assistant. DISHA drafts an answer from official sources; a mentor or official reviews and approves it before the student sees it. Running as a pilot.
Built — needs WAC data
Sign-in with a WAC registration ID: switches on as soon as staff upload WAC's delegate list (or WAC provides a verification API).
Proposed
An ayurworld.org subdomain and mentor-roster sync. These need WAC access.

Three things to know before testing

  1. Nothing reaches a student without a person's approval. DISHA's drafts wait on a staff review desk. Medical questions are never drafted.
  2. Email registrations get the app's own pass number (11WAC/2026/NNNN), not a WAC registration ID. Delegates who sign in with their WAC ID use the WAC ID as their pass (Section 6 — built, needs WAC's delegate list).
  3. The staff access code is shared separately. Never put it in screenshots, emails or bug reports.

2.3 DISHA — the AYURDISHA AI mentor assistant PILOT

DISHA drafts an answer from official sources; a mentor or official reviews and approves it before the student sees it. DISHA is off unless staff switch it on, and only for the career areas they choose.
SafeguardWhat DISHA does
Privacy firstNames, emails, phone numbers, ID-like numbers, pass and ticket numbers are removed before any AI call. No seeker's personal data goes to the AI.
Question checkingSorts each question: on topic, wrong area, unfocused, off topic, duplicate or restricted (medical, exam integrity, self-harm and 6 more). Understands English, Hinglish and Hindi. Self-harm is flagged urgent with helplines.
Medical → mentorMedical and treatment questions are never drafted; they go to a mentor.
Official sources onlyCites only 35 approved websites (NCISM, Ministry of Ayush, NTA, NBEMS, UPSC, state PSCs, WHO, CCRAS, AIIA, ayurworld.org …), with numbered citations.
Claim checkingA second model checks every claim against its source; numbers, dates and fees must appear in it. Thin topics become “Limited sources — mentor to complete” with unsourced figures removed.
Never in the wayDrafting runs in a background queue, so sending a question is never slowed or blocked. A daily spend cap pauses DISHA when reached. Approved answers help with similar later questions.
Section 3

Specifications

LanguagesInterface: English. DISHA understands questions in English, Hinglish and Hindi (the hint, checking and merging handle all three) and drafts in plain Indian English. Content and emails are in English.
Devices & browsersPhones, tablets and laptops; mobile-first. Chrome / Edge 107+, Firefox 104+, Safari 16+ (iPhone and iPad), Chrome on Android. Online only: needs an internet connection.
TechnologyReact 19 + Vite single-page app · Node.js serverless functions on Vercel (Mumbai region) · Google Cloud Firestore database (server-only access) · Google Gemini API for DISHA (Google Vertex AI also supported) · email through the Resend email API or SMTP.
SecurityStaff code checked on the server only, never sent to the browser · secure HttpOnly cookies · every staff action checked on the server · strict security headers (Content-Security-Policy, no framing by other sites) · database denies all direct browser access · demo data and bulk delete disabled in production.
PrivacyNo personal data sent to DISHA. A ticket lookup never shows a name or email. Public boards show no names. No advertising or third-party analytics cookies.
Data collectedRegistration: name, age, sex, institute, email, pass number. Question: the Ask Desk fields (state, stage, institution, self-declared WAC registration and attendance, optional mobile, follow-up consent). Visits: anonymous counts per QR/link tag. Stored in Firestore; visible to staff only.
Key limitsEmail code: 6 digits, valid 10 min, resend after 60 s, 5 tries · question 25–500 characters · group ≤ 7 · seeker session 30 days.
AccessibilityKeyboard use, visible focus, reduced-motion support, text alternatives. WCAG-oriented; not formally audited.
Quality270+ automated unit/API tests and a 40+-step end-to-end journey, all passing; a production probe that cleans up its own test data.
For WAC to decide: age and sex are mandatory at registration; publication consent is recorded automatically (the form does not ask); there is no retention or deletion schedule yet (Section 7).

8Venue connectivityThe app is online only. Reliable Wi-Fi or mobile data at the Meet the Mentors zone and wherever the QR posters hang, so seekers can receive codes and send questions on the spot.

6.2 Sign in with a WAC registration ID BUILT — NEEDS WAC DELEGATE LIST OR API

Built: a delegate enters their WAC ID and the email they used on the WAC site; AYURDISHA's server checks the pair against WAC's delegate data, emails a one-time code to the email on WAC's record, and creates (or links) their profile prefilled from WAC — the WAC ID becomes their pass. Switches on once staff upload WAC's delegate list (Staff → Insights → WAC delegates) or WAC provides a verification API; until then Register shows it as “coming soon”. Email registration is unchanged.

“New here” LIVEUnchanged: email code → AYURDISHA pass number.
✗ Not matchedOne neutral message (never says which field was wrong); limited tries per ID and per network.
⚠ ID already linkedOne WAC ID = one account. A second attempt is blocked and shown to staff.

Columns needed in WAC's export (CSV or XLSX): Registration ID, Name, Email (required); Mobile, Institution, Category (student / delegate / international / faculty …), Country, State, Attendance (in person / online) (optional). Common header spellings are detected automatically (e.g. “Reg No”, “E-mail”, “College”, “Mode”). Re-uploading a newer list updates rows and never touches who has signed in.

Data minimisation: AYURDISHA keeps only those fields — no payment, address or ID-document data. Emails and phones are masked on staff screens by default.

Section 7

What we need from WAC

  • Delegate list (sign-in is built and waiting for it) — WAC's delegate export as CSV / XLSX (Registration ID, Name, Email; optionally Mobile, Institution, Category, Country, State, Attendance) with an agreed refresh, or later a read-only verification API (POST ID + email → match + those fields); plus the WAC ID format with 2–3 dummy examples.
  • Data-sharing agreement — what is shared, purpose, retention and deletion after the programme.
  • Data controller and grievance contact — so Privacy and Terms lose their “Placeholder” label; decisions on mandatory age/sex, explicit publication consent and retention.
  • Approved wording and logo — may the app say “official”? Should the pass number be renamed (e.g. “AYURDISHA pass number”) so it isn't mistaken for the WAC ID?
  • Website owner — a person with WordPress access (button) and DNS access (subdomain), and the chosen subdomain name.
  • Confirmed mentor roster — name, designation, institution, expertise, photo, short bio and each mentor's consent.
  • Programme details — Meet the Mentors schedule and hall layout; talk recordings for the Podcast corner.
  • People — a WAC help-desk contact for delegates, a technical contact, the testers, and the officials who will approve answers.
  • Access policy — keep the app open to all seekers (today), or limit some features to verified WAC delegates?
  • Resources in Section 5 — the accounts and plans listed there, set up on WAC's or the project's billing.
